Mendwell

A clinic and telehealth product. Patients book a video or in-person visit and check in. Clinicians work from the day's schedule and the visit note.

Self-initiated concept design. Names, brands, and figures are fictional sample data, not client work.

What was hard

The hard part of the design is the same visit existing as a booking, a check-in, and a note the clinician can finish.
