Repharbor

Strength coaching with a workout log and a week plan.

Screens

Problem

A coaching program drifts when the workout log and the week's plan are not the same program.

Approach

I designed and prototyped Repharbor so a person logs sets and can send a form-check video, and the coach builds the week from that.

Key decisions

The decision was to keep the log and the plan together.
